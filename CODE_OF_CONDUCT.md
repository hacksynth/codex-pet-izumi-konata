# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our standard / 我们的标准

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable. Critique the contribution, not the contributor.

请保持尊重、具体和建设性。骚扰、歧视、威胁、性化关注、故意干扰以及公开他人隐私信息均不可接受。
请评价贡献本身，而非攻击贡献者。

## Scope / 适用范围

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release comments,
and public spaces where someone represents this project.

本准则适用于仓库 Issue、Pull Request、Discussions、Release 评论及代表本项目的公开场合。

## Enforcement / 执行

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Public reports may
use an issue; confidential reports may use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting form and state that the report concerns conduct rather than security.

维护者可编辑或删除不当内容、关闭讨论、拒绝贡献，或临时及永久限制参与。公开报告可使用 Issue；
保密报告可使用仓库的私密漏洞报告入口，并注明该报告涉及行为准则而非安全漏洞。
